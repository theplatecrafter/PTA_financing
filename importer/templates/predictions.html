How predictions work

Suggestions run when you open an eligible pending transaction or press Run predictions now. Full-transaction rules run first. Account rules prefill their designated fields and combine with model suggestions. Otherwise, the model compares both description and counterparty, payment method/type, category, notes, tags, amounts, installment details, fees, exchange rates, currencies, and original CSV columns.

Sources are kept separate. Field weights start with stronger emphasis on descriptive fields. As different account choices accumulate for a source, fields that distinguish those choices receive more weight. Missing fields do not count as matches. Repeated copies in original CSV columns are suppressed and their total influence is capped.

Dates contribute weekday, month and day-of-month patterns; time contributes time of day. Numbers use relative closeness. Text uses normalized words and Japanese character pairs. Transaction IDs, source file paths and row numbers are excluded. Timing, balances, and amounts alone cannot establish an account suggestion.

The nearest examples must score at least 65% similarity. Voting includes examples within eight percentage points of the strongest match, with at least three supporting the winning account layout and 80% weighted agreement. Agreement is not a probability of correctness.

Only human-confirmed decisions train the model. Corrections replace the saved example; rules and automatic resolutions never train themselves. Parsed-data snapshots are stored alongside the confirmed account layout. Existing drafts and linked events are not predicted.

Simple two-account matches can fill balanced amounts. Fees, splits, and conversions can receive account-layout suggestions, but their amounts remain blank for you to enter. Every resolved transaction still needs valid accounts, currencies and balanced postings.
