PLAIN-TEXT ACCOUNTING, SIMPLIFIED

Every transaction has two sides.

A 25 USD grocery purchase decreases your bank account and increases your grocery expenses.

Assets:Bank−25 USD
Expenses:Groceries+25 USD

Together: 0 USD. That is a balanced transaction.

Transfers move money between your own accounts. Credit card payments reduce a liability; they are not a second expense.

Understand the five account types

Assets: money and things you own.
Liabilities: money you owe.
Income: earnings, usually negative postings.
Expenses: what you spend, usually positive postings.
Equity: opening balances and adjustments.

Account paths use colons, such as Expenses:Food:Groceries. Open accounts on or before your earliest transaction.
